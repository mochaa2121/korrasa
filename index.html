Frequently asked questions

Can I get a refund if the template doesn't work for me?

Yes, within the terms set out in our Refund Policy — in short, if the file is corrupted or clearly not as described, we refund in full. Because these are digital files you can open immediately, refunds for "changed my mind" requests are handled case by case.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cards, Vodafone Cash, and InstaPay, processed through our licensed local payment provider.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Your file downloads directly on the checkout page as soon as payment is confirmed — no account or email required.

What file formats do I get?

This is listed on each template's page before you pay — CV kit and thesis pack are Word documents, and the study planner is an Excel workbook.
